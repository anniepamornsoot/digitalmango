Common questions

Asked honestly, answered honestly.

How much does a custom website cost in Phuket? +
New sites start from ฿35,000. Most projects land between ฿35,000 and ฿80,000 depending on scope. Always a fixed price, agreed up front — no "starting at" surprises.
How long does a website take to build? +
Usually 3–5 weeks from kickoff to launch. The first week is spent understanding your business before any design starts.
Do you build bilingual EN / TH websites? +
Yes — bilingual EN / TH is available as an add-on, priced on top of the base build. It's not a quick translation pass: a second language shapes layout, typography, and tone, so I build it in properly. Tell me on the intro call and I'll include it in your fixed quote.
Will I own the website after launch? +
Yes — the domain, the CMS, the code, all of it. No vendor lock-in, no hostage hosting.
Do you use templates like Squarespace or Wix? +
No. Every site is custom-built from scratch so it actually fits your brand and can grow with your business.
Do you do site refreshes or ongoing care? +
Sometimes, for past clients. Email me and if it's the right fit I'll quote it — but the studio's focus is new builds done properly the first time.
Do you work with clients outside Phuket? +
Yes. I'm based in Phuket and work with clients across Thailand and internationally, in English and Thai.

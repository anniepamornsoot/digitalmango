New sites take 3–5 weeks from kickoff to launch. Refreshes are usually 2–3 weeks. Timeline depends on feedback speed — I'll put a real calendar in the proposal so you can see milestones.

I work in Webflow and custom HTML/CSS for most marketing sites. For e-commerce I'll recommend the right platform for your situation (often Shopify for product-heavy stores, sometimes a lighter setup). I'll always explain why — no upselling for the sake of it.

You provide the words and photos — you know your brand. I'll help shape both to work well on the site, and if you need a copywriter or photographer, I can connect you with people I trust in Phuket.

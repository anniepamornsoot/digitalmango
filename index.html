01 — Hello
I'm Annie.

ripe

ripe ideas, by hand.

I run DigitalMango — a one-person web studio in Phuket. Every project is hand-built, considered, and ships with the care that only comes from a designer who actually does the work herself.

The studio is named after the fruit I think about way too often — sweet, sunny, and a little messy in the best way. That's the energy I bring to every site: warm, easy-going, and uncomplicated, with the polish you'd expect from a serious studio.

I work with cafés, restaurants, and small hospitality brands — businesses that have been quietly running for a few years, and are ready for a website that finally feels like them. Bilingual EN / TH, end-to-end, fixed price.

p.s. — yes, you're hiring me, not a team. that's the whole point.

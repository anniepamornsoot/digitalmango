01How much does a website cost?
Most new sites start at ฿35,000. That's fully fixed — strategy, design, build, bilingual EN/TH, 30 days of post-launch support, all of it. Bigger projects (more pages, booking integrations, complex listings) scale up from there, but you'll always see the full price in writing before we start. No hourly billing. No surprise invoices.
Honest pricing
02How long does it take?
Usually 3–5 weeks from kick-off to launch, depending on how fast we make decisions together and how soon I have your content. I only take on 2 active projects at a time, so when we start, you're not waiting in a queue.
3–5 weeks typical
03I don't have my content / photos ready. Is that a problem?
Not at all — and this is the most common situation. I help you think through what your site actually needs to say, and we figure out together what's worth writing vs. what placeholder works for launch. If you need a photographer, I can recommend ones I trust in Phuket and Bangkok.
We figure it out together
04What if I want to make changes after launch?
You'll be able to edit text, photos, and most content yourself through a light CMS — I walk you through it on the handover call. For bigger changes, I offer a Care & Iter retainer (฿6,000/month) for businesses that want a friendly hand on their site every month. No lock-in, cancel anytime.
You stay in control
05Do I own the website? Can I move it later?
Yes, fully. Domain, hosting, code, content — all in your name, under your accounts. If you ever want to move to someone else, you can. I'm against lock-in by design; the goal is to build you something that grows with the business, not something that traps you.
No lock-in, ever
06Why work with a solo studio instead of an agency?
You talk directly to the person designing and building your site — no account managers, no handoffs, no losing your context between meetings. For small businesses, the speed and the personal investment matter more than a big team. One designer, one number to call, one person accountable.
Human, always

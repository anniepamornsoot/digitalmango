Home/Process

From "hello"
to "it's live."

A simple, transparent process. No surprise invoices, no disappearing between milestones. You always know what's happening and what's next.

01

The free chat.

30 minVideo or in personFree

We have a 30-minute conversation. I ask about your brand, what you're trying to build, where you are now. You ask anything you want. No pitch, no slides, no pressure — just two humans figuring out if there's a fit.

  • You tell me about the business
  • I tell you honestly if I can help
  • If yes, I send a proposal within 48 hours
02

The tasting menu.

2–3 daysPDF proposal

I send you a one-page proposal with clear scope, timeline, and a fixed price. No vague ranges, no "starting at" pricing, no hidden line items. If anything is unclear, we hop on a quick call.

  • Scope, deliverables, and exclusions in plain language
  • A real timeline with milestone dates
  • One fixed price — 50% to start, 50% to ship
03

Sketch & taste.

1–2 weeksFigma2 rounds

I sketch directions, then design the real thing in Figma. You see early wireframes and visual directions before I commit. Two rounds of revisions are built in — most projects only need one and a half.

  • Friday update every week, no exceptions
  • Async feedback in Figma comments
  • One review call per round
04

Build & ship.

1–2 weeksWebflow / custom

I build, test, and launch. Cross-browser checks, mobile QA, speed pass, SEO basics. On launch day we have a handover call where I walk you through editing your own site, plus 30 days of free support after.

  • Loom walkthroughs of every editable section
  • 30 days of "ask me anything" post-launch
  • Optional monthly care plan if you want a hand on it

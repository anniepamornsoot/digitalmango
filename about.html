◐ Phuket, Thailand

Why Digital Mango

I kept seeing the same thing happen to good businesses.

A café owner spends good money on a website. A year later the business grows — new menu, online bookings, a second location — and they're told they have to start over. Again.

A developer builds something custom, then disappears. No handover, no documentation. Just a site nobody else can touch.

A first-time owner, full of ideas and ready to launch, has no clue where to begin — and ends up with a template that looks like everyone else's.

Every time, the problem wasn't budget, and it wasn't the business. It was that nobody sat down with the owner first — before the design, before the build — to understand what the website actually needed to do.

That's the gap Digital Mango exists to close.

How I work

Every site starts the same way — with understanding.

No guesswork, no jumping straight to design. Four steps, every time.

01

Understand

I learn the business first.

I research your market, ask the questions that matter, and study your competitors — what they do well, and where they fall short. Before anything is designed, I know what your customers actually need.

02

Structure

Structure before style.

I map the structure and content layout first — what goes where, and why — built around your customer's real journey, not a template.

03

Design

Clean, modern, made for mobile.

I design minimalist and modern on purpose. Most of your visitors are on a phone with limited patience, so I cut the noise and let them focus on the one thing they came to do.

04

Build

Built, tested, and handed over.

I build it, test it, and hand it over — a site you own and can edit yourself. One person, from first question to launch day.

What I bring

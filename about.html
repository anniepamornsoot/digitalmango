What I believe
Six things I
can't shake.
01

Small brands
deserve great sites.

Quality shouldn't be reserved for big budgets. Every project gets the same care.

02

Slow is
fast.

Two thoughtful weeks beats six rushed ones. Considered design ships fewer fixes.

03

The work
does the selling.

No sales calls. Just careful work and an honest conversation about whether we fit.

04

Templates are
a starting point.

Never a finish line. If your brand has a soul, your site should too.

05

Bilingual
from day one.

EN / TH isn't a translation pass at the end — it shapes layout, type, and tone throughout.

06

You'll always
know what's next.

No disappearing between milestones. A weekly Friday update, every project, no exceptions.

A short timeline
How we
got here.
2022

The studio is planted.

I left a Phuket agency to freelance. First three clients were friends-of-friends — a café, a yoga studio, and a small architecture firm.

2023

The name takes shape.

"DigitalMango" sticks after a client said the work was "sweet and a little messy in the best way." Brand & pricing model lock in.

2024

Booked solid, all year.

Twelve projects, mostly cafés and tour operators. Started bilingual-first as the default rather than the add-on.

2025

Care plans go live.

Monthly retainers for past clients. The Ripe List newsletter ships its first issue.

2026

You, hopefully.

Taking on two new projects in July. The orchard is still small, and that's exactly how I like it.
